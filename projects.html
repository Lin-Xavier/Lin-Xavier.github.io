File Transfer for Android

Copy files between a Mac and an Android phone over USB.

Google no longer supports its Android File Transfer app for Mac, so I built a replacement and improved on it along the way.

It's written in Swift for Apple silicon, with a native Liquid Glass design. It copies files larger than 4 GB, previews files with Quick Look, and if the phone disconnects during a copy, the transfer picks up again once the phone is back.

Novel Manager

Import, organize and read novels on iPhone.

I couldn't find a clean book manager that just did its job, without social features or AI extras bundled in. So I made one.

It's a native SwiftUI app with Apple's Liquid Glass design. When you import a novel, it finds the chapters and shows you a preview first. You can read in scroll or page-turn mode, group books into series with volumes, and browse the pictures in illustrated books. You can also edit a book's text and let the app clean up its formatting for you. Your whole library backs up to one file and can sync privately through your own iCloud.
